Workout Coach

An LLM-powered weightlifting coach for iOS. The wedge is legibility and personal experience: you can see, understand, and edit what the model knows about you — and the coaching is genuinely tailored as a result.

Best viewed on a phone. These are clickable HTML prototypes, not the native app.
